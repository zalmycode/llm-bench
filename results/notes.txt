Notes and caveats
=================

GENERAL
-------
- All GPU runs used -ngl 99 (full offload to GPU).
- Quantization: Q4_K_M only. Do not compare across quantizations.
- pp512 = prompt processing speed (tokens/s), prefill 512 tokens.
- tg128  = text generation speed (tokens/s), generate 128 tokens.
- "not measured" or 0 in pp512 means the run was skipped.

HARDWARE
--------
- RTX 5090 numbers: llama.cpp CUDA backend, driver 570.xx, CUDA 12.8.
- RTX 4090 numbers: llama.cpp CUDA backend, driver 550.xx, CUDA 12.4.
- RTX 4080 Super / 4070 Ti / 3080 / 3060: llama.cpp CUDA backend.
- RX 7900 XTX / 7800 XT: llama.cpp ROCm backend, ROCm 6.2.
- Apple M1/M2/M3/M4: llama.cpp Metal backend, macOS 14/15.
- 2x GPU rows assume --split-mode row tensor split across both cards.
- CPU-only row: Ryzen 9 7950X, 128 GB DDR5-6000, no GPU offload.

SOURCES
-------
- RTX 4090 Qwen2.5-7B tg128 matches a public llama.cpp benchmark.
- RTX 3060 Qwen2.5-7B tg128 matches an Ollama user report.
- M2 Pro Qwen2.5-7B tg128 matches an Ollama benchmark.
- All other numbers are synthetic placeholders for layout testing.
  Replace with your own real measurements before publishing.

KNOWN ISSUES
------------
- Laptop GPUs (mobile variants) are NOT included; their clocks differ.
- Numbers vary +-5% between driver versions; do not over-interpret.
- Q4_K_M on 8GB cards may partially offload; check n_gpu_layers per run.
